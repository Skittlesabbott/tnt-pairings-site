The story behind TnT

It started as snack plates while Troy studied for WSET exams. Tiffany's boards got a little out of hand.

Now it's so much more than bites and books. It's the perfect pair.

By day, Troy runs a steakhouse wine program in Austin

Troy · The wine

Sommelier with years in the wine industry and a knack for the stories that make each bottle special. Troy's favorite thing is matching people with a wine that surprises them.

“There is more variety in wine than there are species on Earth. There's a bottle out there for everyone.”

One of Tiffany's at-home boards

Tiffany · The cheese

Tiffany builds every board around the lineup, balancing flavor and texture so each cheese makes the wine next to it taste better.

“The magic happens when the right cheese meets the right wine. It's chemistry you can taste.”
